# UI design v3: clinical app

- **Status:** Proposed (awaiting review)
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Boards:** `design/v3/` (open `design/v3/index.html` in a browser)
- **Replaces as visual source:** `design/mhai-clinical-mockup-v2.html`. v2
  stays as the checklist of what the app must contain; v3 is how it looks
  and behaves.

## 1. Goal

A modern, calm, intuitive clinical app in the Measured Health brand. It
keeps everything the approved v2 mockup and the Phase 1 Build Guide
require, and adds the screens a real deployment needs: accounts, the
corrections review queue, error and empty states, and export.

**Success means:**

- Every v2 mockup feature is present (section 9 is the checklist).
- A first-time clinician can submit a question and read a result
  without training.
- Confidence is never shown by colour alone.
- Escalated and degraded results always show the review banner and no
  confidence level.
- It works from 1024 px desktop down to 390 px mobile.

## 2. Direction

The style is a blend of three things:

- **Soft health** for the overall feel: rounded cards and gentle gradients.
- **Brand-forward clinical** for the surfaces: a frosted card with a
  gradient hairline border, a faint grid behind the content, and bold
  display headings.
- **Minimal precision** for the Copy action.

Confidence is a labelled pill with signal bars, not a ring or a
percentage. A ring reads as a probability, which the score is not.

The sidebar is light (white to a soft lilac). Purple is used as an accent
only.

### Shape and depth

- **Radius:** 22 px for cards, 12 px for inputs, pills fully rounded.
- **Card shadow:** `0 24px 50px -28px rgba(70,3,81,.4)`.
- **Card border:** a 1 px gradient hairline, mint fading to transparent,
  done with a mask.
- **Background:** a 26 px grid at about 4% opacity, masked to fade out,
  plus soft radial glows (mint at the top right, bloom at the bottom left).
- **Focus ring:** a mint border plus a 4 px ring at 13% opacity.

### Motion

The live demos are in `design/v3/motion.html`. Motion shows progress,
links cause to effect, and confirms actions. It is never decoration.

**Tokens:**

| Token     | Duration | Used for                            |
| --------- | -------- | ----------------------------------- |
| `instant` | 100 ms   | Button press                        |
| `fast`    | 160 ms   | Hover, focus rings, tags            |
| `base`    | 240 ms   | State changes, dialogs, cross-fades |
| `slow`    | 400 ms   | Sheets, result reveal               |
| `story`   | 700 ms   | Meters filling, first load          |

**Easings:**

- **ease-out:** `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)`, the default.
- **in-out:** `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`, for exits.
- **spring:** `cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)`, for small things only (dots,
  checks, the nav indicator, sheets, toasts).

**Patterns:**

- **Result reveal:** parts fade up in reading order, 80 ms apart, and the
  signal bars grow in.
- **Analysing steps:** the active step pulses. When it completes, the dot
  pops, a check draws in, and the progress bar eases forward (driven by
  the API's `progress`).
- **ECG line:** loops only while analysing.
- **Source tags:** hovering a tag highlights its source card, which nudges
  toward the text.
- **Confirming an action:** a button press scales slightly; the
  confirmation pops, its check draws in, and the other buttons dim.
- **Bottom sheet and toast:** slide in with a spring. A toast shows a
  time-left bar and pauses on hover.
- **Skeleton to content:** a cross-fade. No skeleton under 200 ms.
- **Admin numbers:** count up once. Never in clinical results.
- **Nav indicator:** slides between items.
- **Invalid field:** one short shake, then the message fades in.
- **Brand moments:** the "M" draws itself once on sign-in and first load;
  the live dot breathes slowly.
- **Screen changes:** a 16 px slide and cross-fade (View Transitions API).

**Never animate:**

- diagnosis text, doses, or numbers in results;
- safety banners after they appear: no loops or pulses;
- anything over 700 ms;
- parallax or tilt on clinical content.

**Reduced motion:** every animation becomes an instant state change.

## 5. Screens

Each item below names the board and tab it appears on.

### 5.1 Clinical flow (`desktop-tablet.html`, `mobile-clinical.html`)

1. **New consultation.** The form has three numbered groups:
   - **Patient:** age; sex as a segmented control; priority as three
     cards (Routine, Urgent, Emergency).
   - **Presentation:** symptoms as tags, with suggestions; the clinical
     question, with a hint and a character counter.
   - **Optional:** specialty, and additional context (medical history)
     in a collapsible section.

   ⌘↵ submits. The right panel shows "How it works", an example of a good
   question, and example questions to try. On mobile the groups become 3
   steps with a progress bar.

2. **Analysing:**
   - the ECG line;
   - six plain-language steps with timings: done, active and to-do;
   - a progress bar showing the step and the percentage;
   - the right panel echoes the question.
3. **Result: High confidence.** Top to bottom:
   - the ICD-10 chip (hover explains it, click copies it) and the
     confidence pill;
   - the diagnosis in display type;
   - a summary, then next steps as bullets with inline source tags;
   - the actions **I agree**, **Suggest correction** and **Copy ▾**;
   - the completion time, the source count and the disclaimer.

   Hovering a source tag highlights the matching source in the panel.

4. **Use with caution:**
   - an amber banner says why in one sentence;
   - unverified claims carry an amber "Needs review" tag;
   - Suggest correction opens inline, inside the card.
5. **Needs review:**
   - a red banner: "This result needs clinician review before any
     clinical action is taken";
   - no confidence pill, and the diagnosis is labelled "Possible
     assessment · not verified";
   - removed claims are shown struck through with a red "Removed" tag;
   - the reduced-accuracy notice appears when it applies.
6. **Couldn't complete:**
   - the inputs are kept;
   - **Try again** is the main action, with Edit question beside it;
   - a system-status link;
   - the "some services are running slowly" banner when the backend
     reports a degraded state.
7. **Feedback sent:**
   - a green confirmation;
   - both feedback buttons dim and disable;
   - Copy shows "Copied".

### 5.2 Evidence & safety panel

It has three parts:

- **Safety checks:** patient data protected, consistent reasoning, and
  evidence-backed claims with an "n of m" count. Warnings appear inline.
- **Why this confidence:** labelled bars, mint for good and amber for
  weak.
- **Sources used:** each source card has a title, a Verified or Needs
  review badge, an excerpt and a use count.

### 5.3 States and utilities (`states-and-export.html`)

- **Form errors:**
  - errors appear when Analyse is pressed, not while typing;
  - a summary at the top links to each error;
  - each field says how to fix it, and focus moves to the first error;
  - inputs are never cleared.
- **Loading:** skeletons with the same shapes as the real layout.
- **First visit:** a welcome, a "Start a consultation" button and three
  example questions. Choosing an example fills the form.
- **Session timeout:** a countdown dialog at 13 minutes idle, and sign-out
  at 15. A draft stays in memory only.
- **Unsaved draft:** shown when leaving a half-filled form. "Keep editing"
  is the default.
- **404, 403 and offline.** Each explains the problem and gives one action.
  - **403:** points platform admin accounts to the platform admin app.
  - **Offline:** a strip at the top; loaded content stays readable.
- **My corrections:**
  - lists your corrections as waiting, approved or rejected; rejections
    show the reviewer's reason;
  - a toast appears when a decision arrives, and a bell keeps a history.
- **Search (⌘K):** finds consultations by symptom, diagnosis or specialty,
  plus actions.
- **Keyboard shortcuts (?):** N new consultation, A agree, C correct, ⇧C copy,
  E toggle the evidence panel, ⌘P print; in review, A approves and R
  rejects.
- **Help & guide:** how to ask, how to read confidence, how to check the
  evidence, and a glossary of the on-screen labels.
- **Export:** Copy becomes a split button with:
  - copy as text;
  - print or save as PDF;
  - copy the ICD-10 code.
- **Print:**
  - an A4 page that works in black and white;
  - confidence as a word plus bars;
  - numbered sources;
  - the disclaimer and "clinician agreed" in the footer;
  - only what the clinician entered: no names or ids are added.

### 5.4 Corrections review queue (`review-queue.html`)

- **Layout:**
  - the queue on the left; anything waiting over 4 hours is flagged;
  - on the right, what the system said beside the suggested correction,
    with changes highlighted;
  - who suggested it, why, the evidence on file, and a link to the
    consultation;
  - Approve, Reject and Skip in a bottom bar; A and R work as shortcuts.
- **Reject:** a reason is required (quick-pick chips plus a note), and the
  person who suggested the correction sees it.
- **Your own correction:** Approve and Reject are disabled, with an
  explanation, and you can withdraw it instead. This matches the API's
  `403 self_approval`.
- **After approving:** a confirmation with a 10-second undo, then the next
  item loads.
- **Empty queue:** "All caught up", plus your recent decisions.

### 5.5 Account (`account.html`, `account-flows.html`, `mobile-account-review.html`)

- **Sign in:**
  - "Continue with hospital account" (OIDC) first, then username and
    password;
  - on the left, three trust points: patient details are masked, claims
    are checked against guidelines, and the final decision is yours.
- **Two-step code:** six boxes, paste works, and a backup-code link.
- **Sign-in error:** generic wording, and a warning before the account
  locks.
- **First sign-in:**
  1. Acknowledge the terms: decision support only, check flagged items,
     don't type identifiers.
  2. Set up two-step verification with a QR code.
  3. Save the 10 backup codes (download, print or copy), with a
     confirmation tick.
- **Password:**
  - forgot password, check your email, and a new password with a
    strength meter, checked against leaked passwords;
  - "updated" signs out every other device.
- **Account menu:** Profile & security, My feedback, Keyboard shortcuts,
  Help, Sign out.
- **Profile & security:**
  - role, specialty and hospital are locked, because they come from
    hospital records;
  - two-step verification, password, backup codes, active devices and
    "sign out everywhere";
  - feedback stats.
- **No platform admin tools in the clinical app.** Hospital admin tools
  are in it (5.6). See `ARCHITECTURE.md`.

### 5.6 Hospital admin side (`hospital-admin.html`)

A **Hospital admin** group appears in the sidebar for that role: Staff,
Hospital settings, Usage. Roles combine, so a medical director can be
both clinician and hospital admin. Everything is scoped to one hospital,
and none of it shows question text, results or patient details.

- **Staff:**
  - a table of name, roles, specialty, status (active, invited, needs
    attention, deactivated), sign-in method and last active;
  - search, plus filter chips with counts.
- **Invite staff:**
  - one person or a pasted list, with a role and a specialty;
  - with hospital single sign-on, people simply sign in; otherwise they
    get an email invite that expires in 7 days, then first sign-in.
- **Staff member panel (side panel):**
  - change roles and specialty, and set who can review corrections;
  - send a password reset, reset 2-step verification, or deactivate;
  - a warning when hospital policy isn't met, and recent activity.
- **Hospital settings:**
  - review turnaround, and whether reviewers must share the specialty;
  - whether clinicians must use 2-step verification, the idle timeout,
    and "trust this device";
  - SSO status and the allowed email domains (read-only; Measured Health
    configures them).
- **Usage:**
  - counts only: consultations, active clinicians, agreement rate,
    median review time, the confidence mix and the specialty breakdown;
  - CSV export.
- **Activity log:**
  - what happened at the hospital: sign-ins, consultations run, feedback,
    review decisions, account changes, and security events (lockouts,
    policy breaches), which are flagged;
  - a consultation row shows only its specialty and confidence level,
    never question text, results or patient details;
  - filters (person, type, date, flagged only) and CSV export;
  - kept for 12 months.
- **Review oversight:**
  - waiting corrections with their specialty, age and number of eligible
    reviewers;
  - specialties short of reviewers are flagged;
  - **Remind** and **Assign reviewer**. The admin can't approve or reject
    unless they're also a clinician, and sees no clinical content.
- **Guards:**
  - you can't remove your own admin role;
  - destructive actions confirm;
  - every action is audited.

## 6. Wording rules

- Never show backend terms. Use the Build Guide mapping (trust score →
  "Confidence", NLI → "Evidence-backed claims", entropy → "Answer
  stability", PII masking → "Protecting patient privacy", guardrail →
  "Safety checks", KB ids → the guideline name).
- Map decisions to labels: PASS → **High confidence**, REGENERATE →
  **Use with caution**, ESCALATE → **Needs review**, failed →
  **Couldn't complete**.
- Error messages say what happened and what to do next. They never reveal
  whether an account exists.

## 7. Accessibility

- Confidence and status always carry a word and an icon or bar count, not
  only colour.
- Status colours have a target contrast of at least 4.5:1 for text, to be
  checked during the build.
- Everything works from the keyboard, with a visible focus ring;
  shortcuts never trap focus.
- Dialogs trap focus and close with Esc. The default action in a
  destructive dialog is the safe one.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Touch targets are at least 44 px on
  mobile.

## 8. Privacy in the UI

- **No patient data in storage:** not in `localStorage`, IndexedDB or the
  service worker. This includes drafts and session history. The one
  exception is the encrypted offline outbox of unsent questions and
  feedback (see CONTRIBUTING).
- Print or export includes only what the clinician typed, plus the result.
- The timeout clears the in-memory state.
- **Backup codes:** download, print or copy only. There is no direct
  cloud-drive integration, which would add a third-party data flow and
  OAuth scopes; the user saves the file wherever their hospital policy
  allows.

## 10. Backend work this design needs

The frontend leads. The backend will be extended to support this design;
nothing here is a reason to cut a screen. Checked against the
mhai-multiagent API gateway spec (2026-10-08):

1. **Corrections:** reject with a reason, withdraw, and "my corrections"
   (your own, with status and the reviewer's reason). Notifications for
   decisions: polling is enough at first.
2. **Hospitals (tenancy):**
   - a hospital entity, with users belonging to a hospital;
   - a `hospital_admin` role that can be combined with `clinician`;
   - every staff, settings and usage endpoint scoped to the hospital in
     the session.
3. **Hospital admin endpoints** (also: an activity feed with no clinical
   content, plus remind and assign-reviewer actions for corrections):
   - staff list; add (invite one or in bulk); edit role, specialty and
     reviewer rights; deactivate (reversible: the person can't sign in);
     reactivate; and remove (the account is deleted, but past
     consultations, feedback and audit entries are kept and attributed
     under the retention rules). A hospital admin can't deactivate or
     remove themselves;
   - send a password reset, reset 2-step;
   - hospital settings;
   - usage counts.
4. **Password reset by email:** request, emailed link (30 minutes),
   set a new password with a leaked-password check, and sign out
   everywhere.
5. **Sessions:** list active devices, sign out one device, and sign out
   everywhere.
6. **Sign-in:** accept a work email as the login name (the API currently
   uses `username`).
7. **Two-step verification:** a per-hospital policy for clinicians
   (hospital admins, platform admins and auditors always need it),
   backup-code regeneration, and "trust this device" when the hospital
   allows it.
8. **First sign-in:** record that the terms were accepted (version and
   time).
9. **Confidence breakdown:** return all four measures (evidence match,
   internal consistency, values and dosages, answer stability).
   **Until each exists, the UI hides that bar.** It never invents a value.
10. **ICD-10:** `icd10_code` is nullable. The chip hides when it's `null`.
11. **Consultation history:** a persistent, per-clinician history for the
    "Yesterday" and "Earlier" groups and search. This stores clinical
    text, so it needs retention and access rules. Until it exists, history
    is session-only (the Build Guide's Phase 1 rule).
12. **Stage timings:** per-stage durations for the analysing steps. Until
    then, show elapsed time.
13. **Platform admin:** platform admins can add, edit, deactivate,
    reactivate and remove hospitals, configure a hospital's SSO and invite
    its first hospital admin.
    - Deactivate is reversible: the hospital's staff can't sign in and
      their data is kept.
    - Remove is permanent and only through a retention-aware process,
      because hospitals own staff, consultations and audit records that
      can be under legal holds.
14. **Assumed contract for account features.** The frontend calls these
    until the backend defines them, so please match them or tell us what
    changed:
    - `POST /auth/terms {version}`;
    - `POST /auth/password/forgot {email}` (202 for any email);
    - `POST /auth/password/reset {token, new_password}`;
    - `GET /auth/sessions`, `DELETE /auth/sessions/{id}` and
      `POST /auth/sessions/revoke-all`;
    - `trust_device` on `/auth/mfa/challenge`, returning `device_token`,
      which `/auth/login` accepts back;
    - `POST /auth/mfa/recovery-codes`;
    - on the login `user`: `email`, `display_name`, `full_name`,
      `role_title`, `specialty`, `hospital`, `mfa_enabled`,
      `must_change_password`, `terms_accepted_at` and
      `recovery_codes_left`.
15. **Retry-safe writes:** honour `Idempotency-Key` on `POST /queries` and
    `POST /feedback`. A repeat with the same key within 24 h returns the
    first response and does no new work (see item 19 for offline sync).
16. **Compression:** gzip or brotli on JSON responses. A
    `ClinicalResultOut` with citations is the largest payload on a 2G
    link.
17. **Cheaper status checks:**
    - `ETag` on `GET /queries/{id}`, honouring `If-None-Match` with a 304;
    - optionally `GET /queries/{id}?wait=20` (long poll): reply as soon as
      the status changes, or after 20 s.

    Both cut requests and bytes for clinics on slow links.

18. **Tolerant sessions:** a brief loss of connection shouldn't cost the
    session. The 15-minute token with sliding refresh already allows
    this; keep rate limits loose enough for backed-off retries.
19. **Offline sync.** The frontend may send a query or feedback hours after
    it was written (offline outbox, up to 72 h):
    - keep `Idempotency-Key` results for at least 72 h;
    - accept an optional `client_created_at` (ISO 8601) on `POST /queries`
      and `POST /feedback`, so the record shows when the clinician actually
      asked.
