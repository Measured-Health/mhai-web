Hospital admin (inside the clinical app) · v1
The hospital admin's home. Everyone at the hospital is listed with their roles, specialty, status, sign-in method and last activity. Problems surface as filters, for example "2-step not set up". Roles can be combined: a medical director can be both clinician and hospital admin.
Invite one person or paste a list. The invitee gets an email and goes through first sign-in (terms, 2-step setup, backup codes). With hospital single sign-on on, they just sign in with their hospital account.
Click a row to open a side panel. You can change roles and specialty, reset 2-step verification (they set it up again at next sign-in), send a password reset, or deactivate. Every action is recorded in the hospital's activity log. You can't remove your own admin role.
Rules that apply to everyone at this hospital, within limits Measured Health sets: correction review turnaround and who can review, 2-step policy, session timeout, and single sign-on status. Sign-in configuration is shown read-only. Measured Health sets it up.
How the hospital uses MHAI, in counts only. No patient details and no question text. It helps leads see adoption, how often results need review, and correction turnaround.
What happened at the hospital, newest first. It covers sign-ins, consultations run, feedback, review decisions, account changes and security events. Rows never show question text, results or patient details; a consultation shows only its specialty and confidence level. Filter by person, type or date, and export CSV for audits. Security events (lockouts, policy breaches) are flagged.
Keep corrections moving. See what's waiting, how long it's been, and which specialties are short of reviewers. You can remind reviewers or assign a specific reviewer, but you can't approve or reject unless you're a clinician yourself. You see the specialty and age, not the clinical content.
