Account screens · v1
Two ways in. "Continue with hospital account" (single sign-on via the hospital's identity provider) comes first, because most clinicians will use it. Email and password is the fallback. The left side says what the product is and why it's safe to use.
Six boxes, auto-advance, and paste works. "Trust this device for 30 days" cuts down on repeat prompts on a ward's shared machine only if the hospital policy allows it.
Errors say what happened and what to do next, without revealing whether the email exists. After 5 failed tries, the account is locked for 15 minutes, and the screen says so.
Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Everything about "you" lives here: profile, security, feedback history, help, and sign out.
Name, role and hospital come from the hospital's records, so they're shown locked, with a "request a change" link. Security shows two-step verification, password and active devices, with "sign out everywhere". Clinicians see their own feedback stats; admin tools are not here (admins use a separate admin app).
