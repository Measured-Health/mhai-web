Corrections review queue · v1
The list on the left shows what's waiting; the oldest go red after 4 hours. On the right, a side-by-side compare: what the system said vs the suggested correction, with changes highlighted. You see who suggested it, why, and the evidence. Approve or reject sits in a docked bar. Keyboard: A / R.
Rejecting always needs a reason. Quick-pick chips cover common reasons, plus a free-text note. The person who suggested it sees your reason, so the loop closes.
The two-clinician rule is enforced in the UI too: Approve and Reject are disabled on your own correction, with a plain explanation. You can withdraw it instead.
After a decision, a confirmation with a 10-second undo appears, and the next item loads automatically, so you can work through the queue quickly. Approved corrections go on to clinical sign-off before they change the knowledge base.
The empty state is a reward, not a blank page, and shows your recent decisions.
